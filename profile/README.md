# Mode

### Say it, and your phone does it.

Mode is a free, open-source AI agent for your phone. Tell it what you want, by voice or by typing,
and it does it in your apps: it looks at the screen, taps, types, and tells you how it went.

[heymode.app](https://heymode.app) · [Get Mode](https://github.com/heymode/mode/releases/latest) · [Watch the film](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o0WiY1OYjkw)

</div>

- **Works in the apps you already have.** Nothing is written per app. Mode reads the screen and acts on it, the way you would.
- **iPhone and Android.** On Android it can also work in the background while you keep using your phone.
- **Your own AI account.** ChatGPT, Claude or OpenRouter. Your keys stay on your phone, and there are no Mode servers in between.
- **It learns.** How your apps are laid out, your usual order, who "Sam" is. All of it stays on the phone.

Everything is in one repository: **[mode](https://github.com/heymode/mode)**. Issues and ideas are welcome there.
